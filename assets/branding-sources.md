# Institutional identity sources

## KT shell refresh, 10 October 2026

- Brand palette source: https://m.corp.kt.com/html/intro/design/design.html
- Header and navigation use white, black, dark gray (#4C4C4E), and primary brand red (#FE2E36). The logo's existing red is not recolored.
- Header uses a subtle neutral-gray background (#F3F3F3) to separate it from the white navigation. Footer uses black without a red top rule. Network and domain palettes are unchanged.
- KT Flow is not bundled and no licensed webfont file was supplied. Arial is the geometric sans-serif fallback, followed by Helvetica Neue and Noto Sans KR; it is not represented as the official KT typeface.

- Publisher: Frontier AI Lab, KT Corporation.
- Contact: responsible.ai@kt.com, taken from the author block of the HEART Technical Report master, main.tex, lines 83–84, checked on 9 October 2026.
- Official KT corporate identity: https://corp.kt.com/html/intro/design/ci.html
- Official unmodified vector logo: https://cfm.kt.com/images/v2/layout/gnb-ktlogo.svg
- The original SVG is preserved. The transparent reverse variant keeps the original geometry and aspect ratio, with white lettering and the original red ribbon for contrast on navy. No white plate is used. This is a display adaptation, not a claim that KT issued a new asset. KT's logo remains the property of KT Corporation.
