# HEART semantic proximity network

The 622 canonical L4 cards and their 47 L3 assignments remain unchanged. Semantic space is the default home view, with a separate card-explorer tab.

## Reproduction and provenance

Install `scripts/semantic_requirements.txt` in a task-local environment and run `python scripts/build_semantic_network.py`, followed by `node scripts/test_semantic_space.cjs`. The older `build_semantic_space.py` retains historical lexical-projection code and shared scenario rules; its command-line entry now delegates to the current network builder to prevent stale keyword publication.

`semantic_space.json` records the canonical source SHA-256, checked by the browser before rendering. `semantic_embeddings.npz` preserves 1,024-dimensional BGE-M3 vectors, ordered IDs, Ollama model digest and text hash. Inference runs locally using Apple Metal through Ollama 0.32.1; card text is not sent to a hosted inference service. Rebuilding the graph can reuse the published vectors without Ollama or another download. Fresh inference is implemented in `scripts/compare_bge_m3.py`.

## Current pipeline

1. Sort by L4 ID, concatenate the English name and definition, and encode using `BAAI/bge-m3`, packaged as Ollama `bge-m3:latest`. The pinned model digest is `7907646426070047a77226ac3e684fbbe8410524f7b4a74d02837e43f2146bab`. L2 normalisation produces 1,024-dimensional dense embeddings. Requests use `truncate=false`, without an added instruction. Korean translations are displayed but not encoded.
2. Retain the undirected union of eight nearest cosine neighbours per card at similarity >= 0.60. No artificial links are inserted. Edge weights are measured cosine similarity. This exploratory floor preserves the full top-eight graph: the observed minimum candidate score is 0.6218. It is not an expert-calibrated accuracy threshold. Higher floors of 0.65, 0.70 and 0.75 produce 1, 13 and 93 isolated nodes respectively.
3. Weighted Louvain using `networkx.algorithms.community.louvain_communities` in NetworkX 3.5, resolution 1.0 and seed 23, produces 9 communities and 3,807 edges, one connected component and no isolates. Modularity 0.562975 describes graph partitioning, not classification accuracy. Link weights are cosine similarity, calculated as the dot product of L2-normalised vectors: w(i,j) = e(i) · e(j). Louvain is an algorithm name; 3.5 is the implementation library version, not an algorithm version.
4. A seeded weighted spring layout initialises ForceAtlas2, 350 iterations, scaling ratio 8, gravity 0.15, linlog attraction. Uniform centring and rescaling preserve all outliers without clipping. Filters never recompute positions.
5. Colours identify the 47 human-approved L3 categories, anchored in the three L1 domains and varied using L3 semantic relationships as described below. Louvain communities are retained in `graph_communities` as diagnostic metadata, not displayed colour groups. Node strength is the sum of published incident cosine weights. Node area is linearly scaled over the full-network strength range to radii 3 to 15 display pixels: r = sqrt(9 + 216 × (strength − min)/(max − min)). Equal-strength networks use the midpoint area. Sizes remain fixed across filters, including pale inactive nodes. This is not severity, probability or EM confidence.

## Degree-based labels on actual L4 nodes

Hollow centroid anchors and L3 graph labels are removed. Only active L4 nodes whose centres are visible in the viewport are considered for labels. At default zoom, the highest full-network weighted-degree visible node per L3 is a candidate. At 1.5x and 2.5x zoom the allowance grows to two and four per L3; at 3.5x and above EVERY visible active L4 is a candidate, including low-degree and same-L3 terminal nodes. Candidates are prioritised by weighted degree, with L4 ID as a deterministic tie-break. Labels display the L4 English name, wrapped to two lines at overview scales and up to three lines at deep zoom, truncated where necessary. The full name and L3 remain available in the accessible label and hover card. Connecting strokes point to actual existing nodes, not artificial anchors.

Labels are laid out in screen coordinates at a fixed 13px font, trying eight side and diagonal placements around each node. Viewport bounds and collision boxes with six-pixel separation suppress labels that do not fit. Deep zoom removes the L3 quota but does not guarantee that all labels fit in a crowded viewport. Panning, zooming and filtering recompute candidates and placement. Selecting a text label opens the same inline L4 card as selecting its node. Weighted degree describes network connectivity, not severity, typicality or conceptual representativeness.

## Reference analysis

The supplied reference page, its live `assets/risk-space.js`, and local `scripts/build_semantic_proximity_network.py` were inspected. The historical implementation uses BGE-M3, seeded graph-regularised spherical EM, L3-profile similarity (0.65) plus direct semantic similarity (0.35), and ForceAtlas2. Its 54-community claim concerns a different release.

HEART reuses the node-link presentation, weighted connections and network layout, not stale IDs or old EM responsibilities. Current embeddings are recomputed from current HEART text. Links use direct embedding cosine only; diagnostic communities use Louvain, not EM. Displayed colours now use human-approved L3 assignments and L1-anchored semantic shades. No taxonomy remapping is performed. Diagnostic community totals depend on model and graph parameters, not validated counts of risk types.

## Interaction and limits

The three overlapping application lenses, in display order, are AI for Everyone (Korea), Legal assistance AI and Financial chatbots. The delivery-robot and household-humanoid lenses were removed from publication on 10 October 2026; their cards remain unchanged. These are conditional applicability filters, not empirically validated scenario labels. Scenario, L3 category and keyword selections intersect. Inactive cards always remain as pale context; active nodes and the keyboard-accessible list open original cards. L3 categories are collapsed by default with L1/L2/L3 nesting, and Reset filters sits beside the current selection heading. Category labels are clickable. Zoom and background dragging change only the viewport.

Legal assistance AI is grounded in the [Stanford Legal Design Lab 2026 report, 21 August 2026](https://justiceinnovation.law.stanford.edu/ai-for-legal-help-2026-class-report-scoping-building-and-testing-new-legal-aid-tech-systems/). It covers supervised intake, screening, research, document review and drafting, not autonomous adjudication. This is a university project report, not a peer-reviewed evaluation or certification of a deployed service. Financial chatbots is grounded in the [FCA's 2025 LLM consumer-guidance pilots](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/research-notes/money-talks-lessons-2-llm-pilots-consumer-guidance) and [2026 investor-survey findings](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/young-investors-trust-ai-more-tv-or-celebrities). It covers conversational information/guidance, not autonomous trading or lending. These are recent relevant sources identified in the search, not a claim that no newer publication exists. Explicit deterministic rules are preserved in `SCENARIOS`. Generic privacy, reliability, inclusion and accountability families are potentially applicable; associations remain our inference, not risk classifications supplied by the sources.

The AI for Everyone lens replaces autonomous network operations agents on 10 October 2026. Its service scope is based on [Yonhap News reporting of announced plans, 4 September 2026](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20260904048352017): domestic general-purpose chatbots, public and specialised digital agents, information search and delegated applications, bookings and payments via familiar channels. The English label is a descriptive translation, not a verified official name. The unrelated AI learning/development growth-ladder programme is not included. Exact deterministic inclusion/exclusion rules are in `SCENARIOS` in `scripts/build_semantic_space.py`. General and Agentic cards concerning reliability, privacy, harmful output, inclusion and delegated action are included conditionally; Physical-domain cards and explicitly excluded embodied/advanced-autonomy cases are not. These associations are our analytical inference from service capabilities, not claims made by the article or evidence of programme failures.

Links indicate semantic proximity, not causal propagation. Layout distances are not exact embedding distances. Community membership does not establish identical mechanisms, legal equivalence, probability or severity. No new risk cards, revised definitions, assignments or EM scores are generated. Encoder and threshold sensitivity has not been validated as classification accuracy.

# Display baseline

The default view uses a 1.10 display multiplier, equivalent to the previous 110% view. Manual scale buttons and percentage controls are removed. Mouse-wheel and trackpad pinch events zoom directly at the pointer position, preserving the semantic point under the cursor. Wheel delta modes are normalised; zoom is bounded to 0.5–5 times the default and labels are repacked after each change. Background dragging pans, background double-click or keyboard Home restores the default view, and focused keyboard +/− also zooms. Reset filters restores the default view and clears filters. Weighted-degree radii remain unchanged in graph coordinates; displayed diameters are 6.6–33 pixels at the default. Pointer hit testing uses the same multiplier. Wheel gestures outside the graph retain ordinary page scrolling.

## L1-anchored semantic L3 colour model

The English title and definition of each of the 47 L3 categories are extracted from the preserved source snapshots and matched against the Technical Report L3 data master. No L3 is renamed, reassigned or generated. `l3_semantic_colours.json` preserves these inputs and hashes; its named NPZ file stores 47 BGE-M3 vectors and the pinned model revision.

General AI is anchored in blue (#4263eb), Agentic AI in orange (#d87518), and Physical AI in teal (#00977e). Each L1 prototype is the L2-normalised mean of its L3 vectors, with equal weight per L3, not per L4. A category's cosine margin against its nearest competing L1 centroid determines a bounded mixture: other-domain weight = 0.35 / (1 + exp(margin / 0.05)). Mixing occurs in linear-light RGB. The assigned L1 contributes at least 65% to the seed colour; the classification is never replaced by the nearest semantic domain.

Within each L1, two deterministic PCA coordinates of the centred L3 vectors vary hue by at most 34.2 degrees, lightness by 0.10 and saturation by 0.12. Axis signs are fixed by the largest-magnitude component and coordinates are scaled by their maximum absolute magnitude. The L3 seed colours and nearest-L3 mixture metadata remain recorded in `l3_semantic_colours.json`. Earlier diagonal node gradients have been replaced by individual solid L4 colours as described below. This is a reproducible approximate semantic palette, not a distance-preserving embedding into colour space. Unique RGB values do not guarantee that users can visually distinguish every class; names, IDs, tooltips and the hierarchy filter remain essential, including for colour-vision accessibility. Boundary colour indicates semantic proximity, not uncertainty or error in the human-approved classification.

Each L4 uses its existing L3 colour as a seed. Within each assigned L3, three deterministic PCA coordinates of the centred L4 embeddings vary hue by up to 19.8 degrees, lightness by 0.14 and saturation by 0.16. The nearest OTHER L3 definition is selected by L4-to-L3 cosine similarity, with blend weight 0.30 / (1 + exp((assigned cosine - neighbour cosine) / 0.06)). Shortest-arc HLS interpolation mixes that neighbouring seed with the assigned seed before the per-L4 adjustments. Lightness is bounded to 0.30–0.72 and saturation to 0.45–0.95. No random number, ID, degree, probability or severity determines colour. These are approximate semantic shades, not a perceptually uniform distance map. Darker does not mean more severe, frequent or important. Inactive-node opacity remains a separate UI state.

`l4_semantic_colours.json` preserves the 622 solid colours, semantic coordinates, neighbouring L3 and mixing weights. The 3,807 edges, L4 coordinates, node degrees, sizes, label selection, keyword memberships and application memberships are unchanged. Rebuild using `scripts/build_l3_colours.py`, `scripts/build_l4_colours.py`, then `scripts/build_semantic_network.py`; run `scripts/test_l3_colours.py` and `scripts/test_l4_colours.py` for source, palette and unchanged-network checks. Fresh L3 inference requires the pinned Ollama model; subsequent builds use the content-addressed cache. Solid L4 colour inference reuses existing embeddings and requires no fresh model call.

## Source-defined risk keyword retrieval

As of 10 October 2026, risk keywords use documented concept definitions rather than literal-word/L3-prefix membership. Fourteen concepts have primary-source references, short exact quotations and explicit inclusion/exclusion scopes in `keyword_definitions.json`. Definitions are HEART operational syntheses or paraphrases, not claims that each displayed sentence is verbatim source wording. Hate and unfairness is explicitly a composite editorial lens. Definitions of AGI and ASI are conventions, not universally agreed thresholds.

The English concept name and definition are embedded with the same pinned BGE-M3 model and 1,024-dimensional L2-normalised representation used for the L4 names and definitions. Cosine similarity is computed against every one of the 622 unchanged cards. Concept-specific exploratory thresholds and documented AI-specialist scope corrections determine filter membership. Two AI specialist agents reviewed complementary concept groups, and the lead agent adjudicated the proposals and added a missing privilege-compromise check. This is not a new human-review round or independent inter-rater reliability study. The corrections and their reasons are retained in the definitions artifact. Thresholds are not calibrated classification accuracy or a guarantee of exhaustive recall. General AI, Agentic AI and Physical AI instead use exact existing human-approved L1 assignments.

Selecting a keyword displays its operational definition, scope, short attributed quotation and clickable primary-source document. `keyword_semantics.json` retains all 622 cosine scores per semantic concept, selected IDs, model revision and source/definition hashes. Scores are diagnostic retrieval data and are not displayed on L4 cards. Neither keyword retrieval nor graph communities change the master taxonomy.

Reproduce with the semantic runtime by running `scripts/assemble_keyword_definitions.py`, `scripts/build_keyword_semantics.py`, then `scripts/build_semantic_network.py`. The first query embedding run requires the pinned local Ollama BGE-M3 model; later runs reuse a content-addressed query cache. Specialist source and mapping review inputs are stored in `data/keyword_review/`. Run `scripts/test_keyword_semantics.py` and `node scripts/test_semantic_space.cjs` for non-visual numerical and interface-data checks.
