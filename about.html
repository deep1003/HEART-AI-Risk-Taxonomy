1. Overview

HEART, Hybrid Evidence-based AI Risk Taxonomy, links risk definitions to source evidence. Its hierarchy runs from L0 Responsible AI through L1 operating domains, L2 risk areas and L3 categories to specific L4 cards. The dataset contains 622 cards across 47 L3 categories: General AI (492), Agentic AI (67) and Physical AI (63). System, Interaction and Societal Impact are the three conceptual L2 areas, represented by seven populated domain-specific branches.

AI risk space explores semantic relationships. Risk cards combines the taxonomy tree with searchable bilingual cards. Both views use the same human-approved taxonomy. The brand header scrolls away; only the exploration navigation stays pinned.

2. How to use AI risk space

Filter risks

Choose one keyword or application above the graph. Another choice replaces it; choosing the same button again clears it. The active count updates inside the graph, while inactive nodes remain pale. Definitions and at least two supporting source links appear inline. General, Agentic and Physical AI use human-approved L1 assignments; other filters are exploratory relevance mappings, not new classifications.

Reset filters clears filters, selected-node highlighting and the viewport zoom and pan.

Zoom and pan

Scroll or pinch over the graph to zoom around the pointer; drag its background to pan. Double-click the background to reset the viewport without clearing filters. More L4 labels appear as you zoom in, where space allows. For keyboard navigation, focus the graph and use + or − to zoom and Home to reset.

The graph height adapts to the browser viewport. Zooming may deliberately move nodes outside the frame; reset to return to the overview.

Inspect a risk card

Hover over an active node for its ID, English name and definition, L1–L3 hierarchy and weighted degree. Click a node or label to open its full card on the right. The graph narrows without being covered; zoom, pan and selection are preserved. Long card content scrolls inside the panel. On narrow screens, the panel moves below the graph.

Selecting another node replaces the content and resets the panel's internal scroll. Close selected card restores the graph width without clearing zoom, pan or highlighting. Label buttons support Enter and Space.

Browse Risk cards

Use the Risk cards tab for the integrated taxonomy tree and full listings. Select an L1, L2 or L3 branch, or search by ID, name, definition or evidence. On smaller screens, Open taxonomy navigator reveals the tree. Cards include bilingual definitions, optional Facet and Act-type attributes, and evidence references. Probability and Severity remain unassigned.

3. Method and interpretation

Links and position. English L4 names and definitions are encoded as L2-normalised, 1,024-dimensional BGE-M3 dense embeddings using Ollama 0.32.1. The undirected graph takes the union of each node's eight nearest neighbours, retaining cosine similarities ≥ 0.60. Link weight is cosine similarity; ForceAtlas2 supplies the two-dimensional layout.

Colour and size. Blue, orange and teal anchor General, Agentic and Physical AI. The 47 L3 titles and definitions and within-L3 L4 semantic variation produce related shades and bounded boundary mixtures. Node size reflects weighted degree, the sum of incident link weights. Weighted Louvain (NetworkX 3.5, resolution 1.0, seed 23) remains diagnostic metadata, not a replacement for the taxonomy.

Limits. Position, links and boundary shades indicate approximate semantic proximity, not causal relationships or assignment uncertainty. Darker or larger nodes do not imply greater probability, severity or importance. Keyword descriptions are source-grounded syntheses, not verbatim or universally agreed definitions. Filters are exploratory aids; assess actual capabilities and deployment context. Navigation never changes the canonical dataset.
