Method and interpretation

Current English L4 names and definitions are encoded locally by BGE-M3 through Ollama. Dense 1,024-dimensional embeddings are L2 normalised. Cosine-weighted nearest-neighbour links define a graph and ForceAtlas2 places nodes. Colour follows each card's human-approved L3 assignment. The 47 L3 titles and definitions use the same model; three fixed L1 colour anchors, bounded cross-domain blends and within-domain PCA variations encode approximate semantic relationships. Boundary shades do not mean assignment uncertainty. Node area reflects weighted degree, not risk severity.

Application communities remain overlapping editorial lenses. Links and paths express semantic proximity, not causal propagation, probability or severity. Louvain partitions remain diagnostic metadata only. Filtering and colouring never change the original taxonomy.
