AI risk incidents

In preparation. This page will track newly reported AI incidents and analyze them with HEART. Collection has not yet started, and no incident results are available.

Concept and scope

An AI risk is a potential source of harm; an AI incident is a reported real-world event in which an AI system caused or contributed to harm. Near misses, in which harm was narrowly avoided, will be recorded separately, following the AI Incident Database's treatment of harms and near harms.

Coverage will span General, Agentic, and Physical AI, including system failures, misuse, and harmful interactions affecting people, organizations, society, or the environment. Hypothetical scenarios, benchmark results, and unsupported claims will not be treated as confirmed incidents, and allegations or uncertain AI involvement will be labeled as such.

Planned analysis

Each report will be verified, deduplicated, and mapped to the relevant HEART domain, risk area, category, and risk cards. Records will retain source links, event and reporting dates, affected parties, reported harms, and the rationale for each mapping. The analysis is intended to support learning from failures and identifying mitigation needs; it will not estimate incident prevalence or establish causality.

Primary sources

The planned evidence base is the AI Incident Database (AIID), maintained by the Responsible AI Collaborative and listed in the OECD.AI catalogue. Original reports will remain linked for verification, and coverage will reflect the availability and timeliness of public reporting.
