Method

Embedding retrieval followed by semantic adjudication

Criteria were not assigned by a fixed similarity cutoff. Sentence embeddings narrowed the candidates, and final decisions compared the risk mechanism, protected object, and resulting harm.

Reading the scores. Rank and score describe candidate retrieval only; they are not probabilities, severity estimates, or mapping confidence. A 0.80 threshold reached 100% precision but only 17.9% recall on reviewed pairs, so it was used only to prioritize review.
Risk reformulationEach control-oriented criterion was rewritten as the risk that arises when the control fails.
Bilingual representationKorean and English names and statements were embedded with BGE-M3 (1,024 dimensions).
Weighted retrievalSimilarity weighted Korean–Korean pairs at 0.60, English–English pairs at 0.30, and each cross-language direction at 0.05.
Expert reviewReviewers examined the top candidates and the full inventory by mechanism, protected object, and harm, and could add a lower-ranked direct match or abstain.
Fixed inventoryNo L4 card was created, deleted, merged, renamed, or reassigned for this mapping.

Partial mappings

Memory isolation maps only to cross-application data exfiltration, not benign session contamination. Kill-switch coverage maps only to an agent resisting shutdown or correction, not to the absence or technical failure of a kill switch.

Abstention

Resource exhaustion was left unmapped because the current inventory has no card for unbounded loops or tool calls by a single agent; no nearby but semantically different card was substituted.
